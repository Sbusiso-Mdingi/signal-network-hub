# What Sequrin is

Sequrin is a healthcare claims-integrity platform being developed to help medical schemes and other authorised healthcare claims stakeholders identify suspicious patterns, investigate claims more effectively, and understand relationships that may be difficult to see when data is analysed in isolation.

The system is designed around the idea that healthcare claims-integrity risks can exist across:

* claims;
* healthcare providers;
* members;
* facilities;
* organisations;
* time periods;
* behavioural patterns;
* and relationships between these entities.

Sequrin can combine analytical models, deterministic rules, statistical methods and network relationships to produce **investigative signals**.

A detection result is **not a fraud verdict**.

This distinction is central to the product.

Sequrin should never be described as automatically:

* rejecting claims;
* withholding claims payments;
* stopping benefits;
* blacklisting providers;
* declaring someone fraudulent;
* determining guilt;
* imposing sanctions;
* or making autonomous consequential decisions.

Instead, Sequrin helps authorised claims-integrity professionals identify **questions worth investigating**.

The website should communicate the product principle:

**Detection creates a signal. Investigation establishes context. Humans remain responsible for consequential decisions.**
