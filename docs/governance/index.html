Governance

Built for investigation, not automated judgement.

Claims-integrity systems operate in a sensitive domain. Sequrin is being designed so analytical detection supports accountable people rather than becoming the authority for consequential decisions.

01

Signals are not verdicts

A suspicious pattern is something to investigate. It is not proof of fraud or misconduct.

02

No automated claims sanctions

Detection is not authorised to reject, withhold, redirect or otherwise determine claims or benefit-payment outcomes.

03

Human accountability

Evidence, context and consequential decisions remain with authorised human and organisational processes.

04

Governed access

Sensitive claims-integrity information is intended to be available only to appropriately authorised users within controlled organisational boundaries.

05

Auditability

Important investigative and administrative actions are designed to be traceable.

06

Controlled collaboration

Cross-organisational sharing is intended to occur through separately governed processes rather than informal publication or a shared blacklist.
