For medical schemes

Give claims-integrity teams a broader investigative view without surrendering control.

Sequrin is being designed around the operational concerns of medical schemes: limited investigative capacity, sensitive data, organisational boundaries and accountable decision-making.

01

See more than the individual claim

Relationships across providers, members, facilities, timing and other claims can reveal patterns that isolated records may hide.

02

Prioritise investigative capacity

Analytical signals can help teams focus attention on activity that warrants closer examination while preserving human judgement.

03

Keep investigators in control

Detection does not become an automated fraud verdict or payment instruction. Evidence and context remain central to investigation.

04

Protect organisational boundaries

Scheme access, workforce identity and tenant-scoped operational data are designed to remain governed and organisation-bound.

05

Make important work auditable

Access, investigation history and important administrative actions are designed to support accountability rather than opaque automation.

Pilot, onboarding and integration details will be developed separately once the customer journey is addressed page by page.
