How it works

A signal should start an investigation, not make a decision.

Sequrin separates analytical detection from consequential claims decisions. Data becomes context, analysis becomes a signal, investigators establish evidence, and accountable people remain responsible for what happens next.

Tenant-scoped data · Analytical signals · Human investigation · Governed decisions

The operating path

Five stages, with a deliberate boundary between detection and decision.

01

Connect

Relevant claims and integrity data enter a tenant-scoped analytical environment. Organisation, claim, provider, member and other useful identifiers give later analysis a consistent frame of reference.

02

Detect

Rules, statistical methods, models and relationship analysis examine activity for patterns that deserve attention. Different analytical methods can contribute evidence without becoming a decision-maker.

03

Signal

Relevant analytical output becomes an investigative signal. A signal explains why something deserves a closer look and preserves the distinction between suspicion and a finding.

04

Investigate

Authorised investigators review claims, connected entities, chronology, relationships and supporting evidence in a structured workspace.

05

Decide

Consequential conclusions remain with accountable human and organisational processes. Sequrin does not itself reject, delay or withhold a claim, impose a sanction, or publish a shared-network notice.

Where authority changes

Analytics can prioritise attention. They do not inherit decision authority.

Analytical layer

What Sequrin can surface

Unusual claim behaviour or frequency patterns.

Relationships across providers, members, facilities and events.

Signals produced by rules, statistical methods or models.

Context that helps investigators decide where to look more closely.

Human and governance layer

What requires accountable judgement

Establishing whether activity has an innocent, operational or concerning explanation.

Assessing evidence and recording an investigative conclusion.

Taking consequential action through the scheme's authorised process.

Approving any future governed network publication or cross-organisation action.

Detection creates a reason to investigate. It does not create a fraud verdict.
Analytical capability is separated from payment, sanction and publication authority.

From signal to case

The signal carries context forward.

An investigator should not have to reconstruct the analytical story from scratch. The useful context behind a signal can move with it into the investigation workspace, where evidence and human reasoning take over.

01

Pattern detected

An analytical method identifies behaviour that differs from the expected context.

02

Signal generated

The pattern is captured as an investigative lead with the reason it surfaced.

03

Context assembled

Relevant claims, entities, chronology and relationships are brought together for review.

04

Investigator reviews

An authorised person examines evidence, records notes and determines what further work is justified.

05

Outcome governed

Any conclusion or consequential action follows the organisation's authorised human process.

Governance runs through the workflow

Control is not a final step. It surrounds every step.

Organisation boundaries

Claims-integrity work remains scoped to the authorised organisation and its governed operating context rather than becoming an unrestricted shared pool.

Role-based access

Access to sensitive investigative and administrative capability is determined by Sequrin's internal roles, permissions and delegated-authority model.

Traceable actions

Important investigative and administrative activity is designed to remain auditable so the path from signal to action can be examined later.
