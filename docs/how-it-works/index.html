How Sequrin works

A governed path from data to investigation.

Sequrin is structured so analytical methods help people decide what deserves investigation without becoming the authority for consequential claims decisions.

1

Connect

Relevant claims and integrity data enter a governed analytical environment with stable organisation, member, provider and claim identifiers.

2

Detect

Rules, statistical methods, models and network analysis identify patterns worth examining.

3

Signal

Analytical output becomes an investigative signal: a prompt to look closer rather than a finding of misconduct.

4

Investigate

Authorised investigators review evidence, relationships, chronology and context.

5

Govern

Consequential decisions remain with accountable human and organisational processes, while access and important actions remain controlled and auditable.
